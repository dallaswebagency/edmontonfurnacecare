Why Replacing Your Furnace Filter Isn’t Enough

Debris and build-up in your ducts and air vents

Replacing your furnace filter is important, but over time debris and dust accumulate in your ductwork and air vents, leading to decreased airflow, reduced efficiency, and potentially harmful air pollutants circulating through your home. This is also referred to as “duct cleaning.”

Increasing air quality is a whole house issue, not just at the furnace

The air that flows through your ducts and vents circulates throughout your home. Dust, pollen, pet dander and other debris can aggravate allergies and asthma symptoms and contribute to respiratory issues in every room.

Clean your A/C coils if they’re attached to your furnace

If your unit has both a furnace and an air conditioner built together, cold and warm air pass through both the A/C coils and the furnace, so we clean your air conditioning coils at the same time for maximum comfort and energy savings.

At Edmonton Furnace and Carpet Care we specialize in comprehensive ductwork and furnace cleaning to help you maintain the efficiency and performance of your HVAC system. We provide the best furnace cleaning in Edmonton, AB.

Edmonton Dryer Vent Cleaning

We clean dryer vents for residential homes and business owners.

We offer professional dryer vent cleaning for residential customers, businesses and apartment/condo building management companies in Edmonton, Alberta. We begin by inspecting the vent to assess its condition and determine the best cleaning method.

We then use high-powered vacuums, compressed air with a power whip attachment, and other tools to thoroughly clean the vent so all the lint and debris are removed. We also check for any damages or leaks and provide recommendations for repairs if necessary.

A clogged dryer vent makes your dryer work harder, using more energy and raising your utility bills. Regular cleaning saves money on energy and extends the life of your dryer.

Read More

Did you know?

Many people are unaware that not having a clean dryer vent can lead to fires. As lint builds up, it can block the flow of air and cause the dryer to overheat. Lint is highly flammable and can easily ignite when overheated.

    What do I need to do to prepare my house for a furnace cleaning?

    All you need to do prior to our arrival is ensure that we can easily access your furnace and all vents in your home (clearing furniture away from vents or removing anything that may be near your furnace). Let us worry about the rest!

    What is the importance of regular carpet cleaning?

    Many homeowners wait to book a cleaning until carpets have spots or stains, or traffic areas appear worn. Over time, even carpets in low-traffic areas collect dust and soils below the surface where regular vacuum cleaning may not reach. Most carpet manufacturers recommend professional cleaning every 12-24 months for regular traffic areas.

    What do I need to do to prepare my house for carpet cleaning?

    Prior to your appointment, please remove furniture from carpeted areas that you wish to have steam cleaned and ensure the carpets have been recently vacuumed.
